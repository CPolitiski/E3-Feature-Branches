Portrait Design Plan

A — Geometry

Create a portrait of a nervous looking character using 100+ visible geometric shapes.

Use circles to show freckles on the face.

B — Assets

Combine at least two distinct images into one portrait.

Use images found from the internet to create a cracked texture onto a portrait

Place the face in the center and position the texture

C — Color and Curves

Use a gradient to shade the character's face, transitioning from a warm pale color to a cool blue or purple.

Use a Bézier curve to draw a curved smile.

Keep the remaining portrait details simple so the gradient and curved smile stand out.

Selection Criterion

Choose the alternative that best communicates the character's personality and remains recognizable at small sizes.
